How it works

Three crumbs. One second brain.

From forgotten PDF to glowing memory — everything happens on your device, never in a cloud.

Drop in PDFs, textbooks, manuals, notes and research papers. CRUMB treats every page like a fresh slice of memory bread.

02

Your documents are indexed, summarized and imprinted into searchable knowledge — right on your device. Nothing leaves it.

03

Ask, learn, remember

Questions are answered from your vault, with flashcards and quizzes baked in. All of it works with the internet switched off.
